I do not want this website to be merely a profile page. I want it to gradually become a place where research questions, reading notes, project records, and still-unfinished ideas can accumulate over time.

A personal website gives these materials a stable home. It does not need to be perfect from the beginning. What matters more is that it can evolve with me: as my research interests become clearer, the homepage can change with them; as a project becomes more concrete, its page can grow as well; and when a vague thought develops into a mature research note, it can move from a private draft to a public article.

In the future, this process will be managed through Personal OS. Private material will grow inside the system first, and only content that I deliberately select, review, and approve will be published to the public website.
